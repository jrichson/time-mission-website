            <div class="section-badge">Mission Format</div>
            <h2 id="missions-answer-title">How do Time Mission rooms work?</h2>
            <p class="geo-answer-lead">Time Mission rooms are fast, replayable challenges that let teams try physical, logic, skill, and coordination missions in any order while building a score across the venue.</p>
            <details class="geo-disclosure">
                <summary>Read the full mission-room guidance</summary>
                <p>Time Mission mission rooms are <strong>short, challenge-based environments</strong> where teams move through quick <span class="copy-emphasis">physical, logic, skill, and coordination tests</span> instead of staying in one escape room for a full hour. Each room is designed for <strong>repeat play</strong>: players can try a challenge, learn the pattern, improve their score, and continue to another room. The format works for <span class="copy-emphasis">first-time visitors, families, date nights, friend groups, school groups, and team events</span> because the missions vary in intensity and difficulty. Some challenges reward communication, some reward timing, and others reward observation or problem-solving. Sessions are booked by location, and <strong>team size, age rules, accessibility details, and available packages</strong> can vary by venue. The easiest way to choose is to start with your nearest Time Mission location, review the current mission lineup, and reserve a session online today.</p>
